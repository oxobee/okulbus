Key Features

Interactive Map

Monitor your child's bus live with high-precision tracking.

Arrival Estimates

Never miss a stop. View estimated arrival times (ETA) for your bus stop.

Boarding Status

Get instant updates when your child boards or deboards the bus.

Trip Monitoring

Ensuring safety beyond tracking. Admins can access medical profiles during active trips.

SOS Alerts

Drivers can send instant SOS signals to school administrators during critical incidents.

Verified Network

Only verified users can access the system, ensuring data is private and secure.
